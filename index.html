WHY PEOPLE TRAVEL

More than a holiday.

Holidays, family connections, work and education all bring people to Australia. The quarterly mix changed during the pandemic; the annual snapshot shows the balance today.

THE TAKEAWAY

Tourism is a movement of people.
Its impact depends on the trip.

Arrival counts capture recovery. Destinations, purposes, nights and spending reveal how that recovery is experienced across Australia.
